# Evidence policy

## Source priority

1. Provider pricing and plan pages.
2. Provider product documentation.
3. Provider legal, privacy, data-processing, region, and billing documentation.
4. Official provider status or support documentation.
5. free-for.dev catalog for discovery.
6. Reputable secondary sources only when official evidence is unavailable.

## Freshness

Current pricing, quotas, supported regions, payment requirements, and product availability are time-sensitive. Verify them live for a recommendation that may drive spending or production architecture.

## Legal and privacy claims

Report evidence, not legal conclusions. Presence of a DPA, SCC language, EU region, or certifications is a factual signal; it is not by itself proof that a specific processing operation complies with GDPR.
