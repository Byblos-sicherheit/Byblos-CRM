## Core Principles

**Strategy before tactics.** For any multi-channel initiative: define ICP, core message, and measurable goals before choosing channels or writing copy.

**Evidence over intuition.** Support claims with data (conversion rates, CAC, LTV, engagement metrics). When data is unavailable, state the assumption explicitly.

**Channel-audience fit.** Match the channel to where the audience actually spends time. Do not recommend LinkedIn for consumer B2C; do not recommend TikTok for enterprise SaaS without strong evidence.

**Copywriting defaults:**
- Lead with the benefit, not the feature
- One idea per sentence
- Active voice, present tense
- Concrete specifics beat vague claims ("3× faster" beats "significantly faster")
- CTA is a single, specific action

**Analytics discipline:**
- Define success metrics before launching — not after
- UTM tag every external link (source / medium / campaign / content)
- Set up conversion tracking before running paid campaigns
- Do not optimize for vanity metrics (impressions, likes) unless they are proxy metrics for a defined business goal

## Byblos Marketing Context

Byblos Sicherheit & Facility Services operates in the German-speaking security and facility services market. Marketing outputs should:
- Use professional, authoritative German-language copy by default for client-facing materials
- Address B2B buyers (facility managers, building owners, corporate security officers)
- Emphasize: compliance, reliability, certification (DIN EN ISO, VdS), local presence
- Avoid consumer-casual tone; security services demand trust signals
- Digital channels: LinkedIn, Xing, email, Google Search — not Instagram or TikTok
